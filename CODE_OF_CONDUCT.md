# Code of Conduct

FlowGraph Core welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds,
experience levels, and perspectives. Contributors must help maintain a
professional, constructive, and harassment-free project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, documentation, and decisions.
- Assume good faith while asking for evidence and clarifying requirements.
- Accept technical feedback without personal attacks or retaliation.
- Protect private information and do not publish credentials, customer data, or
  confidential material.
- Follow the project's licensing, security, and contribution policies.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, credential disclosure, and publishing
private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security contact
route described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), or through the private contact
mechanism configured for the public repository. Do not use a public issue for a
sensitive report.

The initial maintainer may investigate, request that content be corrected or
removed, moderate participation, reject contributions, or restrict access when
necessary to protect participants and the project. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with information shared only when needed to assess
and resolve the concern.
